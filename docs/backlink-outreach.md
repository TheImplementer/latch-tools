# Backlink outreach kit — Latch Tools

Ready-to-paste ammunition for the **P4** item of the JXL cluster SEO plan. This is the off-page
half of the work: nothing in the repo can create a backlink, and for a young/low-authority domain a
handful of relevant links outweighs almost any further on-page tweak. On-page for the JXL cluster is
already strong (unique schema, HowTo/FAQ, real prose, indexed, in the sitemap) — the bottleneck is
authority and trust, which links and time buy.

**Who runs this:** you do. Everything below is copy to paste and targets to submit to — pick a few
per week, keep it low-volume and genuine.

## Show HN

**Title (pick one):**

- `Show HN: Latch – file converters that run entirely in your browser, no upload`
- `Show HN: Private, in-browser HEIC/WebP/AVIF/JPEG XL converters (files never leave your device)`

**Body:**

> I got tired of "free" converter sites that make you upload personal photos, passports and ID
> scans to a server you know nothing about — so I built the opposite. Latch Tools does every
> conversion 100% in your browser. No backend, no upload endpoint; the tools keep working with
> your Wi-Fi off.
>
> It covers the usual image formats (HEIC, PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF) and also JPEG XL in both
> directions — decode `.jxl` to something your browser can actually open, and encode to `.jxl` for
> storage. Heavy codecs are WASM, lazy-loaded on first use, so pages stay light. There's a short
> explainer on why `.jxl` files won't open in most browsers here: https://latchtools.com/jpeg-xl/
>
> Stack: Astro static output on Cloudflare Pages, @jsquash WASM codecs, no third-party scripts that
> can touch file data. Happy to answer anything about the client-side approach or the codec
> tradeoffs.
>
> https://latchtools.com/

**Etiquette / timing:**

- Post from your own account, once. Weekday mornings US-time (roughly 7–10am ET) tend to have the
  best odds; avoid weekends.
- Reply to every comment, especially skeptical ones about the "no upload" claim — the DevTools
  Network tab showing zero file bytes leaving is your best proof; invite people to check.
- Don't ask for upvotes anywhere — that gets posts flagged. Let it stand on the privacy angle.
- A Show HN that lands is itself a strong backlink and usually seeds AlternativeTo/round-up mentions.

## Reddit & communities

Low-volume, genuine, value-first. Read each sub's self-promo rules; most tolerate a real maker
sharing a free tool once, but hate drive-by links.

- **r/webdev** — the client-side/WASM engineering angle (share what you learned building it, link
  secondary).
- **r/InternetIsBeautiful** — the home page as a clean, free, no-catch tool.
- **r/privacy / r/privacytoolsIO** — the "no upload, works offline" promise, aimed at people
  converting sensitive documents.
- **r/apple / r/iphone** — HEIC→JPG and the JPEG XL angle land well with the Apple crowd (JPEG XL
  opens on Apple devices), when a relevant "how do I convert…" question comes up. Answer the
  question first, link second.

Format that works: answer a real question or share a build note; the link is context, not the point.

## Why this over more on-page work

From the SEO plan: `jxl-to-png` sits ~position 39 despite strong on-page. On a ~1-month-old domain
that's a **trust/authority + internal-link** problem, not a content defect — so rewriting converter
copy won't move it. A few relevant, varied-anchor backlinks (plus domain age accruing over the
coming weeks) is the lever. Ship a couple of these per week rather than all at once; a natural,
gradual link velocity reads better than a spike.
